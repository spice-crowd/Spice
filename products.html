Primary Source Region

From the Heart of Kolli Hills & Across India

Nestled in the Eastern Ghats of Tamil Nadu, Kolli Hills rises to over 1,300 metres — a lush, misty sanctuary where spices grow slowly, develop deeply, and carry the breath of the mountain in every harvest. We source our finest black pepper, coffee, cinnamon, and cloves directly from these storied estates, supplemented by regional partners across India's best spice belts.
